Codolio Identity Integration

Codolio does not currently expose a supported third-party OAuth/OIDC authentication flow.

🛡️ Supported Codolio Sync Flow:
1. Sign in using Google, GitHub, or Email/Password.
2. In your CareerTwin Profile settings, enter your public Codolio profile handle (e.g. codolio.com/profile/username).
3. CareerTwin AI automatically imports your verified LeetCode, Codeforces, and CodeChef ratings.
